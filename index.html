About Us

A Sharjah favourite, one fresh catch at a time

EST. 2023 Serving Sharjah

Tucked into The Mira Building on Sheikh Salem bin Sultan Al Qasimi Street, Asmak Al Khan has built its name on one simple promise: the freshest fish, grilled and served the way seafood should be.

Our kitchen follows an Egyptian-inspired seafood tradition — bold spice, open-flame grilling, and rice cooked low and slow to soak up every flavour. Guests keep coming back for the relaxed, family-friendly dining room, spacious seating, quick and attentive service, and the kind of quality that regulars describe as some of the best seafood they've had in the UAE.

Whether you're settling in for a long family lunch or ordering ahead for takeaway, we treat every plate the same way — fresh, generous, and made to share.

Signature Dishes

What we're known for

A few of the plates our regulars order again and again. The full menu is even bigger — see below for how to browse it.

Asmak Al Khan Mixed Grill

Our signature platter — grilled fish, char-grilled shrimp and golden calamari rings, served over fragrant spiced rice. Built for sharing.

Grilled Jumbo Shrimp

Jumbo shrimp, marinated and grilled over open flame until smoky and tender. A favourite for those who want seafood, unapologetically.

Whole Fried Fish & Rice

A whole fish, fried to a crisp golden finish and brightened with fresh lemon, plated with calamari rings and our house rice.

Seafood Soup

A light, fragrant broth loaded with crab, shrimp and mussels — the way we like to start a proper seafood meal.
